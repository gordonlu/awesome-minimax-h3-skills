# Clay Café Stop-Motion — Cozy Claymation

Use this Skill when the user wants a **15-second cozy claymation café short** that feels like a premium handcrafted stop-motion advertisement: a coffee shop scene rendered in warm, cute paper-clay and ceramic textures with soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field, and delicate macro cinematography.

Core principle: **the camera stays mostly fixed with a gentle, slow macro breathing push-in, maintaining generous white space and the original composition.** All objects keep their original soft paper-clay and ceramic model textures, with slight frame-by-frame stop-motion rhythm that feels natural, refined and healing. The palette stays ivory, beige, warm wood-brown, sage green and terracotta — low saturation throughout.

The prompt follows the three-part H3 structure (core concept → timecoded beats → hard negative rules). I2VA: one reference image (locks the composition and visual style; not used as the first frame unless explicitly requested).

## When to use
- Claymation / stop-motion café scenes
- Warm, cozy, healing atmospheres with tactile textures
- Handcrafted miniature world aesthetics
- Premium stop-motion advertisement style

## Hard rules
- The café architecture, furniture, plants, signage, coffee machine, display case and large ivory paper background stay identical to the reference.
- All objects maintain paper-clay and ceramic model textures with subtle frame-by-frame stop-motion rhythm.
- Camera is mostly fixed with only very slight, slow macro breathing push-in; maintain generous white space.
- Soft natural warm-white daylight; ivory/beige/sage/terracotta low-saturation palette throughout.
- Hand-drawn scrapbook doodles (hearts, stars, coffee beans, steam lines, arrows) occasionally pop/flicker/extend then restore — like living scrapbook doodles.
- No structural changes to the shop, no new characters, no costume changes, no text distortion.
