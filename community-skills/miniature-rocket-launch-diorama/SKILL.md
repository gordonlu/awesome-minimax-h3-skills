# Miniature Rocket Launch Diorama

Generate a 10-second miniature diorama rocket launch video from a single reference image.

## What it does

Preserves the exact rocket design, launch tower, red service structures, lattice towers, support buildings, roads, sandy terrain, and handcrafted architectural-maquette appearance from the reference image. The camera remains completely fixed in an elevated 3/4 isometric composition.

## Animation timeline

- **0–3s**: Rocket lifts off beside the launch tower with brilliant orange-white exhaust flame. Dense white smoke expands around the launch pad.
- **3–7s**: Rocket climbs above the tower. Exhaust plume lengthens. Smoke spreads around the base, flowing around buildings.
- **7–10s**: Rocket reaches upper frame. Exhaust trail extends downward. Ground-level smoke continues billowing and dispersing.

## Secondary motion

Small flags flutter from exhaust disturbance, tiny loose dust near the pad is pushed outward, smoke curls around launch structures, engine light produces subtle warm reflections on nearby metal surfaces.

## Key constraints

- Fixed camera throughout (no pan, tilt, zoom, push-in, orbit)
- Maintain handcrafted miniature-model appearance
- Rocket stays perfectly rigid and structurally consistent during ascent
- Launch tower and entire diorama base remain fixed and geometrically stable

## Input requirements

- **1 reference image** (I2VA): The exact rocket launch diorama to preserve
- Or pure text description (T2VA)

## Installation

```bash
copy install miniature-rocket-launch-diorama
```
