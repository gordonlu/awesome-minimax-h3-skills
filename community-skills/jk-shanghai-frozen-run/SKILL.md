# Anime JK × Real Shanghai — Bullet-Time One-Shot

Use this Skill when the user wants a **15-second 16:9 mixed-media short that is ONE continuous unbroken shot**: a clean 2D anime JK schoolgirl living inside an otherwise photorealistic, documentary-style modern Shanghai street. She runs urgently through real pedestrians, trips on a curb, and at the very first instant of the fall TIME FREEZES while the same handheld camera smoothly becomes a precise bullet-time rig that orbits her at waist-to-head height — threading through her suspended school bag, textbooks and instant-photo prints — then widens, returns to the original angle, and time resumes as she catches herself on one knee.

Core principle: **the anime girl and everything from her bag stay 2D; the city, pedestrians and vehicles stay photorealistic.** The camera is a single physical trajectory (MiniDV handheld → early-fall freeze → horizontal bullet-time orbit → close passes through book/bag objects → wider orbit → return to side angle → time resumes → MiniDV handheld). During frozen time the girl is the orbit center and the camera stays around waist-to-head height; only the camera moves.

The prompt follows the three-part H3 structure (core concept → timecoded beats → hard negative rules). I2VA: one character reference image (locks the girl's face/body/outfit/anime style), used only as reference (never as the first frame).

## When to use
- Mixed-media shorts: anime character × real-world environment
- Bullet-time / frozen-time orbit around a single subject
- Continuous one-shot camera choreography (no cuts, no montage)
- Early-2000s MiniDV handheld feeling inside a realistic city

## Hard rules
- The JK girl and her belongings (bag, textbooks, notebooks, worksheets, pencil case, instant-photo prints) remain clean 2D anime; the Shanghai street, pedestrians, vehicles and architecture remain photorealistic live action. Never convert either side.
- Preserve the girl's exact appearance from the reference across running, falling, close-ups and the frozen orbit (no redesign).
- Freeze VERY EARLY in the fall (torso only moderately tilted, still clearly above ground); the orbit begins immediately and stays around waist-to-head height with the girl as center.
- One continuous camera trajectory, no teleporting or cuts; on resume, everything continues from the exact frozen state.
