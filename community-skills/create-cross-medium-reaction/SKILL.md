# Create Cross-Medium Reaction — Sticker Dog vs. Real Night Market

Core principle: **one real hand at a time, a perfectly flat sticker dog, and every reaction reads as a clean cross-medium gag — no 3D volume, no realism in the sticker, no continuity breaks.**

The prompt follows the H3 timecoded structure (core concept → five-segment screen description with positive and negative per beat → hard negative list). T2VA only — pure text, no reference image.

## 5. Hard Rules

- **One live-action hand at a time** — exactly one live-action hand visible, and it must never share a frame with another hand.
- **Hand duty split** — the left hand never stir-fries and the right hand never holds the jar; the left hand never touches the spatula; no live-action hand helps 旺财 pour chili.
- **Perfectly flat sticker** — 旺财 must stay a completely flat 2D sticker: no 3D volume, no realistic fur, no volumetric light, no plastic depth, no clay texture, no realistic shadow; only mild flat-cartoon squash-and-stretch. No text, numbers, logos, jewelry or extra accessories on the character.
- **One continuous take** — fixed wide composition, only slight handheld drift, no cuts, no transitions, no zoom, no major re-composition.
- **Silent clip** — no dialogue anywhere.

## 6. Negative Rules (Do Not)

3D volume or realistic fur/shadow/clay texture on the sticker; two live-action hands in any frame; two left hands or two right hands; duplicate arms; extra palms; a second spatula; a continuity break of one spatula in the wok and another in the air; the left hand stir-frying; the right hand holding the jar; the left hand touching the spatula; a live-action hand helping 旺财 pour chili; text/numbers/logos/jewelry/extra accessories on the sticker; realistic pain (gagging, coughing, burning); dialogue.
