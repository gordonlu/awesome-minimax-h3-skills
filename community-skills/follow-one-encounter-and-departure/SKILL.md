# Follow One Encounter and Departure — 1970s 16mm Street Chase

Use this Skill when the user wants a **cinéma-vérité street follow**: a 16mm film camera tailing one person through a living 1970s city street, catching an encounter, then letting them dissolve back into the crowd.

Core principle: **the camera stays unsettled and invisible-friendly until the last second — raw grain, shoulder-held shake and pure natural light carry the truth of the street, never modern digital sharpness.**

The prompt follows the H3 timecoded structure (core concept → four-segment screen description with positive and negative per beat → hard negative list). T2VA only — pure text, no reference image.

---

## 1. Trigger

Use this Skill when the user's request matches any of:

- A street-follow / documentary-style long take after one person
- "Make it look like 1970s analog 16mm film"
- A cinéma-vérité (realism) observational short — one encounter, then departure
- A period New York (or any 1970s city) street atmosphere piece

Do **not** use this Skill when:

- The user wants modern digital HD sharpness or a stabilized gimbal follow
- The user wants a fully scripted, posed scene — this must feel improvised and observed
- The user wants music or voice-over — only on-location sound is allowed
- A reference image must drive the look (this is pure T2VA)

## 2. Inputs

- A text description of the scene only: 1970s New York, Greenwich Village streets and a small square; a twenty-something completely make-up-free young woman in a long coat and printed dress walking through, brushing past children, and vanishing into the moving street.
- Duration 15s, aspect 4:3, 24fps, raw 16mm film look.
- No reference image, no first frame — T2VA.

## 3. Core Concept

15 seconds, 4:3, documentary short. A 1970s New York Greenwich Village street piece shot like a 16mm cinéma-vérité follow — a twenty-something completely make-up-free young woman walks through streets and a square, tailed closely by the lens, brushing past a group of children, before dissolving into the moving street. Raw 16mm film texture, heavy organic grain, pure natural daylight, shoulder-held camera with heavy shake, no stabilization, occasional focus loss, slight light leaks, real and elegant, rich with daily life; the frame stays unsettled until the very end. Ordinary cuts.

## 5. Hard Rules

- **Unsettled frame until the end** — the camera never locks down; it stays shaky, improvised and observant throughout.
- **Raw 16mm film texture** — heavy organic grain, pure natural daylight, shoulder-held heavy shake, no stabilization, occasional focus loss, slight light leaks.
- **4:3 framing** — keep the 4:3 aspect and the 16mm grain at all times.
- **On-location sound only** — no music; only street ambience (street voices, children calling, pigeon wings, footsteps, wind and distant traffic).
- **No on-screen text** — clean frame; no subtitles, no logo.

## 6. Negative Rules (Do Not)

Subtitles or Logo; music; modern digital image quality; any text on screen; stabilized shots; posed eye contact; crisp sharp digital detail; a cleared empty square; modern street elements; falls or conflict; posed third-person staging; a lens that settles down before the end; adding any modern elements.
